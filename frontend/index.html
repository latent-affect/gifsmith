Transcribe — audio → timestamped dialogue (local)

Extracts the audio track and transcribes + diarizes it entirely on this machine (whisper.cpp + sherpa-onnx — nothing is uploaded anywhere, ever). The dialogue arrives as timestamped chunks: treat the transcribed text as a timing placeholder, write your own script in the dashed boxes, then use Import from Transcribe on the Convert tab.

Runs entirely on your machine. Server binds to 127.0.0.1 only. Warning threshold defaults to the mean of verified GIF-autoplay platform limits (X 15 · Discord 10 · Tumblr 10 · GIPHY 100 · Mastodon 16 ⇒ ≈30 MB); change it with the server's -warn-mb flag.
